## Annex 0.4.3

A Windows PC's picture no longer freezes a few seconds into a session on a slow connection.

### Fixed

- **A Windows PC's picture froze about ten seconds in.** On a slow connection, often over the internet through the relay, the picture and the mouse from a PC stopped, and the PC couldn't end the session, not even on Quit (it said sessions took too long to close). When the connection was slow, the PC's video encoder asked for a fresh picture just as the session was changing the encoder's quality, and each waited for the other. They no longer wait on each other, so the picture keeps going and its quality follows the connection.
